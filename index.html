How Scoring Works

FAQ

Where do the scores come from?

From each NFT's own metadata on Robinhood Chain, read through the public Blockscout explorer. If the metadata includes a Score or Points value, that number is used. Otherwise the 7 item traits are scored using the collection's point table.

Does my score update when I buy or sell?

Yes. The checker reads current ownership every time you spin, so a pile you just bought counts right away and one you sold drops off.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

No. You can paste any address. "Use my wallet" only reads your address from MetaMask or another browser wallet. It never asks you to sign or send anything.
